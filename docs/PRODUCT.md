# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hiring managers, founders, design leads, and potential collaborators evaluating Sai Bhanupalla for product, frontend, and creative-engineering work. They arrive skeptical and time-poor, usually from a link in an email, DM, or job application, often on mobile. The job to be done: within ~15 seconds, decide "is this person legit and interesting enough to keep reading / reach out?" The site has to answer "what do they make, how good is it, and what's it like to work with them" without making the visitor dig.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that functions as proof-of-craft, not a resume. Because this is a developer + creative profile, the site itself is the strongest work sample: how it looks, moves, and holds together is the argument. Success = the visitor remembers it, believes the skill claims because the page demonstrates them, and takes one action (email, view a project, follow a link).

## Brand Personality

Warm, playful, human - expressed loud. Bold, expressive, and hand-made: anime-manga + punk-zine energy (black/yellow/red, halftone, kanji, torn collage), confident and a little reckless, never corporate or buzzword-y. It should feel like a real person with taste and attitude made this by hand, not a template. Emotional goal: a jolt of energy, then trust. The visitor should grin at the audacity once and trust the craft throughout.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate / safe / forgettable portfolios: muted slate-and-white, centered hero with a stock gradient, three identical feature cards, a timeline, a contact form. Invisible by design.
- LinkedIn-as-a-website energy: dense, formal, achievement-listing, zero point of view.
- The over-used 2026 editorial-magazine look (display serif + italic + ruled columns + tiny tracked metadata) used as a default rather than a deliberate choice.
- AI-default palettes: cream/sand/beige body backgrounds, AI-purple-on-white, navy-cream-with-orange.
- Busy "look how many libraries I know" pages where every element animates on scroll.

## Design Principles

1. **The site is the portfolio.** Every interaction is an audition; craft the page to the standard of the best project on it.
2. **Show, don't list.** Demonstrate skill through the build quality, not adjectives. If a claim isn't backed by something on the page, cut it.
3. **Warmth is a choice, not a default.** Personality comes from voice, accent color, type character, and motion, never from a beige background.
4. **Commit to one strong idea per screen.** Long scroll, deliberate pacing, one dominant idea per fold. No clutter.
5. **Memorable beats safe.** Risk a little strangeness. Forgettable is the only failure mode that matters here.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target WCAG 2.2 AA. Body text >= 4.5:1, large text >= 3:1; verify all text-on-color fills perceptually (white text on saturated fills).
- Full keyboard operability and visible focus states; semantic landmarks and heading order.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honored everywhere: motion degrades to crossfade/instant, never removed entirely in a way that hides content.
- Color is never the only signal; reveal animations enhance already-visible content (never gate visibility on a transition).
